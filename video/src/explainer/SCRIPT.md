# "5 cheezein, 1 raaz": voiceover script (Hinglish, about 49s)

Record in your own voice and save as `public/audio/explainer-voiceover.mp3`.
Then adjust the times in `script.ts` (SCENES, LINES, SFX) to match.

1. AC coach mein aapko paanch cheezein milti hain. Par inmein se ek… har trip ke baad nahi dhulti. Guess karo, kaunsi?
2. Do chadar, ek takiye ka cover, ek towel… aur ek kambal.
3. Jawab hai: kambal! Baaki chaaron cheezein har safar ke baad laundry jaati hain…
4. …dhulti hain, steam press hoti hain, aur naye sealed packet mein wapas aati hain.
5. Lekin kambal coach mein hi rehta hai. Uski dhulai hoti hai kam se kam mahine mein ek baar. Yaani ek hi kambal, mahine bhar, kai yatriyon ke kaam aata hai.
6. Toh smart tareeka kya hai? Step one: pehli chadar berth par bichhao. Step two: doosri chadar kambal ke andar daalo, bilkul cover ki tarah. Step three: takiye par cover chadhao.
7. Ab aapko sirf fresh chadar chhuegi, kambal nahi.
8. Aur kuch trains mein ab kambal par printed cover bhi aata hai, jo alag se dhulta hai.
9. Toh yaad rakho: chadar har trip, kambal mahine mein. Aise hi explainers ke liye Mindset Mode Explainers follow karo.
