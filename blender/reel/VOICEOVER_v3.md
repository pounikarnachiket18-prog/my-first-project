# Voiceover v3: the metro metal detector, straight to the point (Just KnowIt)

A direct explainer with no story: the question, the answer in five steps (the same five step titles that appear in
the video), a one-line close and a call to follow the channel, Just KnowIt, over the logo ending. The opening title ("Metro
ki ek machine… Lekin aakhir KAISE?") matches it.

**This is the voice in the reel now.** It is synthesised offline by `tts_voiceover.py` with Kokoro (open-source TTS,
male Hindi voice `hm_omega`), one clip per line, each placed on its shot. To use a human voice instead, record the
plain script below and swap it in; the times in the table are where each line starts.

## Plain script (for reading aloud)

> Metro ka metal detector, jeb ka chhota sa sikka bhi pakad leta hai. Kaise?
>
> Jawab hai, electromagnetic induction. Gate mein do coils hoti hain, transmitter aur receiver. Dono milkar metal ko
> paanch steps mein pakadti hain.
>
> Transmitter coil har second sainkdon current pulse bhejti hai. Isse gate ke beech magnetic field banta hai.
>
> Shareer se field nahi badalta. Lekin metal aate hi, field disturb ho jaata hai. Is badlaav se metal ke andar eddy
> currents ghoomne lagte hain. Ye currents ulta field banaate hain, receiver ise pakadta hai, aur alarm baj jaata hai!
>
> Zone lights batati hain metal kahan hai. Signal jitna strong, metal utna bada. Isliye sikka, belt, bada metal, sabka
> response alag.
>
> Bas, itna sa science.
>
> *(over the logo)* Aur explainers ke liye, follow karo, Just KnowIt!

## Recording notes (for a human re-record)

- **Tone:** clear, confident and conversational, like explaining to a friend. No dramatic pauses.
- **Pace:** about 3 words a second.
- **Emphasis:** stress the key words that also appear on screen: *transmitter*, *receiver*, *magnetic field*,
  *eddy currents*, *alarm*.
- **Timing:** "alarm baj jaata hai" lands around the alarm (0:35–0:37), and "follow karo, Just KnowIt!" on the white
  "FOLLOW FOR MORE" button in the logo ending (about 0:47.5).
