## Plain script (Hinglish, for reading aloud or re-recording)

> Yeh gate toh lagbhag har insaan par BEEP karta hai. Phir security guards ko kaise pata chalta hai ki kisi ke paas
> sikka hai, bandook hai, bomb hai, ya belt? Chaliye samajhte hain.
>
> Asal mein, yeh machine sirf beep nahi karti, balki metal ki maujoodgi aur uske electromagnetic response ko bhi detect
> karti hai.
>
> Is walk-through metal detector ke andar khaas coils lagi hoti hain, jo lagaatar ek invisible electromagnetic field
> banaati rehti hain. Jaise hi koi vyakti iske andar se guzarta hai, uske paas maujood metal is electromagnetic field
> mein badlaav paida karta hai. Is badlaav ki wajah se, metal ke andar chhote-chhote electric currents paida hote hain.
> Machine ke sensors is badlaav ko turant detect kar lete hain.
>
> Phir signal ki strength aur response ke aadhaar par machine ko andaaza hota hai ki metal object kitna bada ya kitna
> significant hai. Isi wajah se system coin, belt, ya kisi bade metal object ke liye alag-alag response de sakta hai.
