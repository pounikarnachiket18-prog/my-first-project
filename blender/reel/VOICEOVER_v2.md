# New voiceover: "Tinku vs the Metal Detector" (Just KnowIt)

A new storyline over the same reel. Tinku Tension (the "AKA Shortcut King" on the ID card in the video) thinks a couple of
coins in his pocket will slip past the gate. The machine explains, step by step, why they won't. The science is the
same as before (transmitter coil, magnetic field, eddy currents, the coin's own field, receiver coil, alarm, zone
lights, signal strength), but it is told as a story with a payoff.

Each line starts where its shot starts, so it can be recorded straight over the existing video. Speech runs from
0:03 to about 0:46 (about 134 words), then the logo ending plays.

## Recording notes

- **Tone:** playful storyteller for Tinku's lines (setup and payoff), clear and confident for the science in the
  middle.
- **Pace:** brisk, about 3 words a second (the same pace as the reel's current narration). The pauses marked "…" are the comic beats; keep them short.
- **Timing:** "pakde gaye!" should land just after the alarm hits (0:35–0:36). The last line runs into the start of
  the logo transition, which is fine.
- **On-screen hook:** it still reads "Airport ki ek machine… Lekin aakhir KAISE?". To match the new story it could
  read "Tinku ne socha, machine ko chakma de dega… / Lekin KAISE pakda gaya?". That's a quick graphics change with no
  3D re-render.
